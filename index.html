About The Artist

The Intersection of Passionate Artistry & Studio Mastery

Based in the historic cultural valley of Kathmandu, Nepal, Prabhat Maharjan is an accomplished singer, composer, multi-instrumentalist, and sound engineer whose work bridges authentic acoustic heritage with cutting-edge global audio production.

Whether performing intimate fingerstyle acoustic guitar ballads, composing sweeping symphonic piano & violin themes, or engineering pristine audio mixes for prominent indie releases, Prabhat brings meticulous dedication to every bar of music.
