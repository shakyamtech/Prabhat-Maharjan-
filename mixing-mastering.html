Audio Preparation & Stems Submission Checklist

Before uploading your audio tracks, please review these technical guidelines to guarantee optimal mastering results:

1

Sample Rate & Bit Depth

Export in 24-bit or 32-bit float at 44.1kHz, 48kHz, or 96kHz. Avoid lossy MP3/AAC formats.

2

Headroom & Dynamics

Leave -6 dB to -3 dB of peak headroom on your master bus. Remove all limiters and heavy compressors.

3

Common Start Point

Export all individual stems starting from Bar 1 Beat 1 (0:00) so they align automatically in the DAW.

4

Reference Tracks

Include 1 or 2 commercial reference songs that represent your ideal acoustic tonal balance and punch.
